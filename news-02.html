Experiments reveal the trade-off between quantum speed and energy cost

← All news

The group and international collaborators experimentally investigated the relationship between quantum evolution speed and energy cost in a diamond solid-state spin system. The study appeared in npj Quantum Information on 28 November 2025.

Using a geometric speed defined by the Schatten 2-norm, the researchers verified a tight bound set by the energy-cost rate. For pure states evolving along a geodesic, the bound can be reached. In closed systems, quantum coherence supplies the contribution to unitary evolution speed.

The study also examined open systems, separating coherent and incoherent contributions introduced by environmental decoherence. These results inform fast quantum control with lower energy consumption. Yang Dong is the first author and Fang-Wen Sun is the corresponding author.
