Our research

Measuring the small.
Understanding the fundamental.

We connect quantum physics, optical techniques and precision engineering to develop new ways of sensing at micro- and nanoscales.

Direction 01

Optomechanical sensing at micro- and nanoscales

Using light to suspend individual nanoparticles in vacuum, we create highly isolated mechanical oscillators for precision measurement and microscopic thermodynamics.

Explore the research →

Direction 02

High-spatial-resolution quantum sensing and imaging

We combine optical imaging, coherent spin control and integrated devices to resolve weak signals at the nanoscale and improve the performance of quantum sensors.

Explore the research →

Direction 03

Coherent control of solid-state spins and applications

We develop precise quantum operations and integrated diamond sensors, balancing sensitivity, accuracy, dynamic range, bandwidth and robustness.
