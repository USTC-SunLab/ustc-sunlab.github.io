Progress in super-resolution wide-field quantum sensing

← All news

The group developed super-resolution wide-field imaging and sensing with diamond NV-center ensembles. The result was published in Applied Physics Letters.

A digital micromirror device generated patterned illumination. Because diamond’s high refractive index can distort these patterns, the researchers used blind structured illumination microscopy to recover the illumination information directly from the measured fluorescence images.

The approach improved wide-field resolution by approximately 1.8 times without requiring the illumination parameters in advance. It was used to characterize the conductive and optical properties of silver nanowire networks.
